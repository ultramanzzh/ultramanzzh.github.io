01 /About me

Background

I am a Researcher at AgiBot (AGIBOT Innovation (Shanghai) Technology Co., Ltd.). My interests lie at the intersection of robotics and artificial intelligence.

My recent work explores how robots can understand task progress through errors and recovery, learn visual representations across embodiments, and track whole-body motion.

I studied at Stanford University as an MS student from 2024 to 2026, following undergraduate studies at Zhejiang University from 2020 to 2024.
